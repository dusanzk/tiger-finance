Support

Tiger - Family Finance. Questions, problems and requests.

Contact

Write to dusanzk@me.com. Say which device you use (iPhone, iPad, Android or the web) and what happened. A screenshot helps. You will get an answer by email.

Getting an account

Tiger accounts are created by the person who runs your Tiger server, so there is no sign-up button in the app. If you were invited, use the username and password you were given. If you need an account, ask the person who invited you, or write to the address above.

Forgot your password

Ask the person who runs your Tiger server to reset it, or write to the address above from the email address on your account.

Delete my account and data

Write to the address above from the email address on your account. Everything in it is removed. You can also delete individual transactions, accounts and files yourself inside the app.

Alfred and your data

Alfred shares your question with xAI to write his answer. On iPhone and iPad he asks first, and Settings, Alfred and xAI turns it off again. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
