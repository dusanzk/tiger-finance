Privacy Policy

Tiger - Family Finance. Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Tiger is a personal and family finance app (iPhone, iPad, Android and the web). It is operated by its developer, whose name is shown on the App Store page, and the developer runs the Tiger server that stores your data. Questions about privacy go to dusanzk@me.com.

What Tiger keeps

  • Your account: username, display name, email address if you gave one, and your settings.
  • What you record: accounts, transactions, amounts, payees, categories, tags, notes, budgets, rules, people and shared-account records, and the receipts, invoices, statements or other files you attach or import.
  • Location, only if you switch it on: where you were when you logged a transaction. It is off until you turn on Location Logging, and you can turn it off again.
  • Your phone's notification address: the code Apple gives Tiger so it can send you notifications, with the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Android) and the app version.
  • Technical logs: when the app hits an error or crashes it sends a short technical log (what went wrong, when, and the app and device version) to the Tiger server so the problem can be fixed. These logs are not tied to your account.
  • Alfred conversations: the messages you exchange with Alfred, Tiger's assistant, and the files you give him.

Tiger does not contain advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or rent your data.

Where it is stored

On the Tiger server run by the developer. Everything travels between your device and the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Face ID, a passcode or a picture password can lock the app on your device, and the account can use two-factor sign-in or a passkey.

Who else receives anything

  • xAI (Alfred only). When you send a message to Alfred, Tiger passes that message, any files you attached to it, and the parts of your books Alfred needs to answer (transactions, amounts, payees, categories, notes and account names) to xAI, an artificial intelligence company, whose Grok model writes the answer. Nothing is sent to xAI unless you send a message to Alfred, and on iPhone and iPad Tiger asks for your explicit permission first. You can take that permission back at any time in Settings; Alfred then stays closed and the rest of Tiger works as before.
  • Apple (notifications). If you allow notifications, Apple's push service delivers them. The notification text, such as the name of the person who sent you something, passes through Apple.
  • Email (optional). If you ask for email digests or reminders, they are sent from the Tiger server's mail service to the address you gave.

Other people with access to a book you share can see what is in that book. That is how sharing works, and you choose who is in it.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Your records stay until you delete them. You can delete transactions, accounts and files yourself in the app; deleted items sit in a recycle bin for a limited time before they are removed, and backups of the whole server are kept for a limited period. To delete your whole account and everything in it, write to dusanzk@me.com from the address on your account and it will be done.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or to stop any use of it, by writing to dusanzk@me.com. You can also export your own transactions from Tiger on the web at any time.

Children

Tiger is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that affects how your data is shared is announced in the app before it takes effect.
